# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Be direct, useful, and respectful. This project is a curated technical index, so discussion should focus on evidence, usefulness, maintenance state, and fit for the Herdr ecosystem.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Spam, affiliate stuffing, or deceptive links.
- Repeatedly resubmitting rejected entries without addressing feedback.
- Misrepresenting unofficial projects as official.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove entries that do not meet the curation standard or make the project less useful.
